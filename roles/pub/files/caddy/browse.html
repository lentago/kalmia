Always-on LAN webserver · Caddy on LXC 114 (pve4) · 192.168.139.9

This is a drop target. Anything placed in the NAS lentago share under web/ is served here live — files render, folders become browsable indexes.

How to publish:

  • From the laptop: write to /mnt/lentago/web/
  • From the runner fleet / PVE host: /mnt/neptune-lentago/web/
  • Inside the container it’s mounted at /srv/www

Drop an index.html in a subfolder and that folder renders as a page; drop loose files and the folder shows a listing like the one below.
